The colors a screen may use. Write the "What to write" column in a component. The numbers are the values for the two test themes.

| Token | Job | What to write | Neutral light | Neutral dark | Okvy light | Okvy dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `color.bg.canvas` | Page background behind all content. | `var(--color-bg-canvas)` | `#f6f6f5` | `#141412` | `#FFFFFF` | `#000000` |
| `color.bg.surface` | Surface for inputs, badges, and grouped specimens. | `var(--color-bg-surface)` | `#eeeeec` | `#232320` | `#F2F2F2` | `#141414` |
| `color.bg.subtle` | Quiet fill behind a secondary action. | `var(--color-bg-subtle)` | `#e2e2df` | `#32322e` | `#E6E6E6` | `#262626` |
| `color.fg.default` | Default text. | `var(--color-fg-default)` | `#141412` | `#f6f6f5` | `#000000` | `#FFFFFF` |
| `color.fg.muted` | Secondary text. | `var(--color-fg-muted)` | `#5e5e58` | `#a3a39d` | `#595959` | `#B8B8B8` |
| `color.fg.onPrimary` | Text on the primary action. | `var(--color-fg-on-primary)` | `#f6f6f5` | `#f6f6f5` | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` |
| `color.border.default` | Default border. | `var(--color-border-default)` | `#c8c8c4` | `#474742` | `#D9D9D9` | `#404040` |
| `color.border.focus` | Focus ring. | `var(--color-border-focus)` | `#5e6a71` | `#5e6a71` | `#000000` | `#000000` |

| `color.action.secondary` | Fill of a secondary action. | `var(--color-action-secondary)` | `#e2e2df` | `#32322e` | `#E6E6E6` | `#262626` |
