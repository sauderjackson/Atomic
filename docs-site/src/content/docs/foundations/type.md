The type roles a screen may use. Write the "What to write" column in a component. The numbers are the values for the two test themes.

| Token | Job | What to write | Neutral light | Neutral dark | Okvy light | Okvy dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `font.family.body` | Body and UI font stack. | `var(--font-family-body)` | `"Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` | `"Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` | `"Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` | `"Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif` |
| `font.size.display` | Largest type size. | `var(--font-size-display)` | `40px` | `40px` | `40px` | `40px` |
| `font.size.title` | Section title size. | `var(--font-size-title)` | `28px` | `28px` | `28px` | `28px` |
| `font.size.body` | Body size. | `var(--font-size-body)` | `16px` | `16px` | `16px` | `16px` |
| `font.size.label` | Label, button, and badge size. | `var(--font-size-label)` | `14px` | `14px` | `14px` | `14px` |
| `font.weight.body` | Body weight. | `var(--font-weight-body)` | `400` | `400` | `400` | `400` |
| `font.weight.label` | Label and title weight. | `var(--font-weight-label)` | `600` | `600` | `500` | `500` |
| `font.lineHeight.body` | Body line height. | `var(--font-line-height-body)` | `1.5` | `1.5` | `1.5` | `1.5` |
| `font.lineHeight.title` | Title line height. | `var(--font-line-height-title)` | `1.25` | `1.25` | `1.25` | `1.25` |
