The corner roles a screen may use. Write the "What to write" column in a component. The numbers are the values for the two test themes.

| Token | Job | What to write | Neutral light | Neutral dark | Harbor light | Harbor dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `radius.control` | Corner radius of buttons and inputs. | `var(--radius-control)` | `4px` | `4px` | `4px` | `4px` |
| `radius.badge` | Corner radius of badges. | `var(--radius-badge)` | `4px` | `4px` | `4px` | `4px` |
