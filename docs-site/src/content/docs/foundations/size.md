The size roles a screen may use. Write the "What to write" column in a component. The numbers are the values for the two test themes.

| Token | Job | What to write | Neutral light | Neutral dark | Okvy light | Okvy dark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `size.tap` | Minimum tap target. | `var(--size-tap)` | `44px` | `44px` | `44px` | `44px` |
| `size.border` | Border thickness. | `var(--size-border)` | `1px` | `1px` | `1px` | `1px` |
| `size.outline` | Focus outline thickness. | `var(--size-outline)` | `2px` | `2px` | `2px` | `2px` |
| `size.icon` | Icon box size. | `var(--size-icon)` | `20px` | `20px` | `20px` | `20px` |
